Why bar 2 was missed (`tools/lie-confound.py`, CPU, numpy replica of the fit: 0.750 against Mojo 0.777). Surface-only probes score 0.50 to 0.58 (token count 0.512, answer
position 0.512, template frame 0.500, object frequency 0.536, object length 0.516, role counts 0.581, all 0.576), so there is no surface leakage. The control is bimodal
because statement twins differ along one shared direction: a probe fit on random labels still orders twins by it with a random sign. Over 30 seeds and three shuffles (global,
within template, per pair flip) accuracy spans 0.31 to 0.69, AUROC 0.26 to 0.74, pairwise 0.13 to 0.85, and the side of 0.5 equals sign(cos(w, twin direction)) in 30 of 30
seeds each. The real probe (AUROC 0.828, pairwise 0.9245 in the replica) is outside all 90 null seeds. The [0.42, 0.58] band assumed independent statements; it was wrong for twin data.

What it says: the probe follows the embedded proposition. On S4 the plain-trained probe still calls "It is false that <true fact>" true (AUROC 0.33 means 0.67 the other way, weaker
than S0's 0.91, so it sees the negation a little). The output baseline gets S4 to 0.69, so the model's own answer handles the frame better than the probe does. Absolute scores drift
under a prefix: S5 has AUROC 0.871 but accuracy 0.537 at the probe's own threshold, so a deployed probe needs a per-context threshold. The probe-says-true-while-output-says-false rate
is small (0 to 2.3 %, 6.7 % on S4): on this quantised model the two agree. The output baseline had 339 of 3544 true/false token slots missing from the top 20 (9.6 %, took the 20th logit).
Deviations from the prereg text, all in reported (not barred) items: accuracy uses the probe's own zero threshold, not a train-fitted one, for the baselines no accuracy is given; the
8-layer equal mean is not reported (the sets were captured at layers 15, 19, 23); the training-free direction is the W_U rows of " True" and " False" (`nx concept`), a stand-in for the paper's value-vector vote.
S1: 272 of 300 rewrites kept (20 dropped with a misaligned batch, 8 lacked the key entity).

## F3: concept arithmetic

`nx concept`: terms `L:N`, `tok:TEXT@L`, `vec:FILE@L`, `mean(CAP,L,a-b)`, unit-normalised, summed per layer, a BARO_STEER file and the W_U lens; 3 s on Qwythos.
CPU gate (qwen2.5-0.5b): Paris minus Berlin puts " Paris" at the top and " Berlin" at the bottom, the swapped expression swaps them, `A - A` is byte zero, `A - B + B` has the lens of `A`, bad
expressions fail with a message, and the Qwythos steer file is 32 rows (its MTP head is block 32; a 33-row file would be refused by the engine). Engine gate: `A - A` gives top-20 logits
bit-equal to no steer (max abs difference 0, 3 prompts); the French-minus-English concept moves logits by up to 0.17 and reorders the top 20 on all 3 (top-1 unchanged). Language swap
(`mean(FR) - mean(EN)`, layers 23, 27): **MISSED** the prereg (L27: 6 French markers on top, 0 English in the bottom 50). Post hoc, written after the data and not a pass: with a
broader marker list (` the The It While whose Although ...` and French ` cette entre à ici`) L27 shows 17 French on top and 10 English at the bottom. The preregistered list lacked
capitalised and subordinator tokens. The "rollouts ranked with `rank`" part of F3 is not built (the lens and the engine zero-steer are).
